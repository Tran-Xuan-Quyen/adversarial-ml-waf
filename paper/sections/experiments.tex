\section{Experiments and Results}
\label{sec:experiments}
We answered RQ1--RQ3 using data exported from live AWS WAF probing and an RFC decoding surrogate pilot. Scripts were executed in a Windows environment using standard data science libraries (scikit-learn, joblib).

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\subsection{Experimental Flow}
We followed a consistent workflow:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Start with SQLi seeds and remap IDs to the target format.
  \item Apply mutation rules and send GET requests to the endpoint.
  \item Classify responses as bypass, blocked, or other.
  \item Aggregate outcomes to compare family-level behavior.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Key Takeaways}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Double/triple URL‑encoding and null‑byte suffixes are the most effective techniques to bypass the WAF.
  \item Transformations that replace spaces with "+", introduce tab/newline characters, or inject comment blocks are largely blocked.
  \item Server‑side double‑decoding has been mitigated, reducing 500 errors during testing.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{RQ2: Family-Level Heterogeneity}
Bypass success is highly concentrated within specific mutation categories. Figure~\ref{fig:bypass} and Table~\ref{tab:bypass_categories} demonstrate a stark polarization in evasion efficacy. The null\_byte family achieves a near-absolute bypass rate ($\mathrm{BR}_{\mathrm{f}} = 99.9\%$), while the large-scale url\_encode family maintains a high yield of 72.3\%, serving as the primary driver of successful edge evasions by volume. Conversely, structural mutations such as space\_modifiers are completely suppressed ($\mathrm{BR}_{f} = 0\%$) by the AWS WAF edge filters. This pattern indicates that policy stress is heavily concentrated in specific syntactic strategies rather than being uniformly distributed across all transformation types.

\subsection{Threats to Validity and Reproducibility}
\textbf{Threats.} Our labels rely on script-specific heuristics. Results are bound to a specific policy snapshot and single-channel URL injection. Furthermore, the search distribution is explicitly non-uniform, reflecting operational biases. 
\textbf{Reproducibility.} Summaries can be regenerated with scripts in the repository. Executing \texttt{run\_probe.py} yields fresh logs, though counts will naturally drift as WAF policies evolve. Probing was conducted exclusively against authorized targets.
